\section*{Course References and Notation}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Course References and Notation}
Throughout, notation follows \emph{Poly cours MB.pdf}.  In discrete time we write
\(\Stilde^i_t=S^i_t/S^0_t\), \(\Vtilde_t=V_t/S^0_t\), and
\(V_t=\langle \theta_t,S_t\rangle\).  In the Black--Scholes model we use
\[
  dS_t=\mu S_t\,dt+\sigma S_t\,dB_t,\qquad S^0_t=e^{rt},
\]
and the risk-neutral probability \(\Q\) under which
\[
  dS_t=rS_t\,dt+\sigma S_t\,dB_t^{\Q}.
\]
References such as \polyref{Eq.~(3.9)} or \polyref{Thm.~1.3} refer to the
course poly.
