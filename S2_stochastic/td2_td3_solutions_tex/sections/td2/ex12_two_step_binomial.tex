\begin{exercise}[Two-step binomial call]
In the two-step binomial model with \(S^1_0=100\), \(u=1.5\), \(d=0.5\),
\(T=4\), \(N=2\), \(r=-\frac12\ln(0.8)\), price the European call with
strike \(K=100\), and fill the pricing and hedging tree.
\end{exercise}

\begin{solution}
Here \(\Delta t=T/N=2\), hence
\[
  e^{r\Delta t}
  =
  e^{-\ln(0.8)}
  =
  \frac{1}{0.8}
  =
  1.25.
\]
By the binomial risk-neutral formula \(\polyref{Thm.~1.2, Eq.~(1.8)}\),
\[
  q=\frac{e^{r\Delta t}-d}{u-d}
   =\frac{1.25-0.5}{1.5-0.5}
   =0.75.
\]
The one-step discount factor is \(e^{-r\Delta t}=0.8\).  At maturity the call
payoffs are
\[
  (225-100)^+=125,\qquad (75-100)^+=0,\qquad (25-100)^+=0.
\]
Backward induction \(\polyref{Thm.~1.3, Eq.~(1.12)}\) gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
  p^G_{t_1}(150)
    &=0.8\bigl(0.75\cdot125+0.25\cdot0\bigr)=75,\\
  p^G_{t_1}(50)
    &=0,\\
  p^G_{t_0}(100)
    &=0.8\bigl(0.75\cdot75+0.25\cdot0\bigr)=45.
\end{aligned}
\]
The hedge ratios are the binomial deltas from
\(\polyref{Thm.~1.3, Eq.~(1.13)}\):
\[
\begin{aligned}
  \theta^1_{t_1}(100)
    &=\frac{75-0}{150-50}=\frac34,\\
  \theta^1_{t_2}(150)
    &=\frac{125-0}{225-75}=\frac56,\\
  \theta^1_{t_2}(50)
    &=\frac{0-0}{75-25}=0.
\end{aligned}
\]

Thus the time \(0\) no-arbitrage price of the call is \(45\), and the missing
tree entries are those displayed above.
\end{solution}
