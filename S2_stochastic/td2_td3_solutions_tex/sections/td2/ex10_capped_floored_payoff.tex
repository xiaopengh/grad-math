\begin{exercise}[A capped and floored payoff]
Consider the derivative with maturity payoff
\[
  H=\min\bigl(\max(S_T,K_1),K_2\bigr),\qquad K_1<K_2.
\]
Plot the payoff and express its time \(0\) price using two call prices.
\end{exercise}

\begin{solution}
The payoff is constant at level \(K_1\) below \(K_1\), equal to the underlying
between \(K_1\) and \(K_2\), and constant at level \(K_2\) above \(K_2\):
\[
  H =
  \begin{cases}
    K_1, & S_T\le K_1,\\
    S_T, & K_1<S_T<K_2,\\
    K_2, & S_T\ge K_2.
  \end{cases}
\]

\begin{center}
\begin{tikzpicture}
  \begin{axis}[
    width=9cm,
    height=5cm,
    axis lines=left,
    xmin=0,xmax=3,
    ymin=0,ymax=2.4,
    xtick={1,2},
    xticklabels={\(K_1\),\(K_2\)},
    ytick={1,2},
    yticklabels={\(K_1\),\(K_2\)},
    xlabel={\(S_T\)},
    ylabel={payoff},
    clip=false
  ]
    \addplot[blue!60!black,very thick,domain=0:3,samples=150]
      {min(max(x,1),2)};
    \addplot[gray,densely dashed] coordinates {(1,0) (1,1)};
    \addplot[gray,densely dashed] coordinates {(2,0) (2,2)};
  \end{axis}
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{center}

Algebraically, for every \(x\ge0\),
\[
  \min(\max(x,K_1),K_2)
  =
  K_1+\pos{x-K_1}-\pos{x-K_2}.
\]
Taking \(x=S_T\), the derivative is therefore replicated by:
holding a zero-coupon bond paying \(K_1\) at \(T\), buying one call with
strike \(K_1\), and selling one call with strike \(K_2\).  By the replication
pricing principle \(\polyref{Def.~1.9 and Prop.~1.7}\),
\[
  \boxed{\ 
  H_0=K_1e^{-rT}+C_0(S,T,K_1)-C_0(S,T,K_2)
  \ }.
\]
\end{solution}
