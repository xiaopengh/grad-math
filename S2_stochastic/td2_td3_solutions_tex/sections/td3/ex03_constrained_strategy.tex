\begin{exercise}[A constrained strategy cannot replicate nonnegative claims]
\textbf{Restatement.}
Let \(G\ge0\) with \(\EQ[G^2]<\infty\).  Show that \(G\) cannot be replicated by
a strategy constrained to hold \(\theta_t=c/S_t\) units of the risky
asset, with \(c\in\R^*\).

\begin{solution}
Assume, toward a contradiction, that a self-financing strategy
\((x,\theta)\) with \(\theta_t=c/S_t\) replicates \(G\).  In discounted
form, under the risk-neutral measure,
\[
  \Vtilde_T^{x,\theta}
    =x+\int_0^T\theta_t\,\dd\Stilde_t
    =x+\int_0^T\frac{c}{S_t}\sigma\Stilde_t\,\dd B_t^\Q
    =x+c\sigma\int_0^T e^{-rt}\,\dd B_t^\Q.
\]
Since \(c\ne0\), the last random variable is Gaussian with mean \(x\) and
strictly positive variance
\[
  c^2\sigma^2\int_0^T e^{-2rt}\,\dd t>0.
\]
Hence
\[
  \Q\left(x+c\sigma\int_0^T e^{-rt}\,\dd B_t^\Q<0\right)>0.
\]
Equivalence of \(\P\) and \(\Q\) gives the same positivity under \(\P\).
But a replicating portfolio would satisfy
\[
  \Vtilde_T^{x,\theta}=\e^{-rT}G\ge0
  \qquad\text{almost surely},
\]
which is impossible.  Therefore no such constrained strategy can replicate
the nonnegative claim.
\end{solution}
\end{exercise}
