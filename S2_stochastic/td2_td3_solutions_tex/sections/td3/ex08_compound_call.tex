\begin{exercise}[Compound call]
\textbf{Restatement.}
Let \(0<T_1<T\).  The payoff at \(T_1\) is a call on the time-\(T_1\) price of a
European call maturing at \(T\):
\[
  G=\pos{C_{T_1}(S,T,K)-K_1}.
\]
Derive the price, express it with a bivariate normal probability, and explain
why it is replicable using only \(S\) and the risk-free asset.

\begin{solution}
Let \(u(t,x)=C^E(t,x,T,K)\) be the usual BMS call price on \(S\) with maturity
\(T\) and strike \(K\).  For \(t\le T_1\), put
\[
  \tau=T_1-t,\qquad \tau_1=T-T_1.
\]
By risk-neutral pricing,
\[
  p_t
    =\EQ\left[\e^{-r(T_1-t)}\pos{u(T_1,S_{T_1})-K_1}\,\mid\,\F_t\right].
\]
Conditionally on \(S_t=x\),
\[
  S_{T_1}
    =x\exp\left(\left(r-\frac12\sigma^2\right)\tau
       +\sigma\sqrt{\tau}\,Z\right),
  \qquad Z\sim\mathcal N(0,1).
\]
Hence \(p_t=v(T_1-t,S_t)\), where
\[
  v(\tau,x)
    =\e^{-r\tau}\E\left[
       \pos{
         u\left(T_1,
           x\e^{(r-\frac12\sigma^2)\tau+\sigma\sqrt{\tau}Z}
         \right)-K_1
       }
     \right].
\]

The function \(x\mapsto u(T_1,x)\) is continuous, strictly increasing, tends to
\(0\) as \(x\downarrow0\), and tends to \(+\infty\) as \(x\to\infty\).  Therefore
there is a unique \(x_1>0\) such that
\[
  u(T_1,x_1)=K_1.
\]
We use the sign convention
\[
  d=\frac{\log(x/x_1)+(r-\frac12\sigma^2)\tau}{\sigma\sqrt{\tau}},
\]
so that
\[
  x\e^{(r-\frac12\sigma^2)\tau+\sigma\sqrt{\tau}Z}>x_1
  \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad Z>-d.
\]
Thus
\[
  v(\tau,x)
  =
  \e^{-r\tau}
  \E\left[
    u\left(T_1,x\e^{(r-\frac12\sigma^2)\tau+\sigma\sqrt{\tau}Z}\right)
    \1_{\{Z>-d\}}
  \right]
  -\e^{-r\tau}K_1\normalcdf(d).
\]

To expand the remaining expectation, introduce an independent
\(Z_1\sim\mathcal N(0,1)\).  Conditional on \(S_{T_1}\), the time-\(T\) stock is
\[
  S_T
  =
  x\exp\left(
    \left(r-\frac12\sigma^2\right)(\tau+\tau_1)
    +\sigma(\sqrt{\tau}Z+\sqrt{\tau_1}Z_1)
  \right).
\]
Using the call pricing formula inside \(u(T_1,\cdot)\) gives
\[
  v(\tau,x)+\e^{-r\tau}K_1\normalcdf(d)
  =
  \E\left[
    \left(
      x\e^{\sigma(\sqrt{\tau}Z+\sqrt{\tau_1}Z_1)
        -\frac12\sigma^2(\tau+\tau_1)}
      -K\e^{-r(\tau+\tau_1)}
    \right)\1_A
  \right],
\]
where
\[
  A=
  \left\{
    \sigma(\sqrt{\tau}Z+\sqrt{\tau_1}Z_1)
    >
    -\left(\log(x/K)+\left(r-\frac12\sigma^2\right)(\tau+\tau_1)\right)
  \right\}
  \cap\{Z>-d\}.
\]

Now use the notation from the statement
\[
  N_2(y,y_1,\rho)=\P(Z<y,\ Z+\rho Z_1<y_1),
  \qquad
  \rho=\sqrt{\frac{\tau_1}{\tau}}.
\]
Define
\[
  a_1=\frac{\log(x/K)+(r+\frac12\sigma^2)(\tau+\tau_1)}
            {\sigma\sqrt{\tau}},
  \qquad
  a_2=\frac{\log(x/K)+(r-\frac12\sigma^2)(\tau+\tau_1)}
            {\sigma\sqrt{\tau}}.
\]
By symmetry of \((Z,Z_1)\),
\[
  \P(A)=N_2(d,a_2,\rho).
\]
Completing the square in the exponential term shifts
\((Z,Z_1)\) by \((\sigma\sqrt{\tau},\sigma\sqrt{\tau_1})\), which gives
\[
  \E\left[
    \e^{\sigma(\sqrt{\tau}Z+\sqrt{\tau_1}Z_1)}
    \1_A
  \right]
  =
  \e^{\frac12\sigma^2(\tau+\tau_1)}
  N_2(d+\sigma\sqrt{\tau},a_1,\rho).
\]
Therefore
\[
  \boxed{
  v(\tau,x)
    =
    x\,N_2(d+\sigma\sqrt{\tau},a_1,\rho)
    -K\e^{-r(\tau+\tau_1)}N_2(d,a_2,\rho)
    -K_1\e^{-r\tau}\normalcdf(d)
  }.
\]
Taking \(\tau=T_1-t\) gives the time-\(t\) price \(p_t=v(T_1-t,S_t)\).

Finally, the payoff is a measurable function of \(S_{T_1}\):
\[
  G=\pos{u(T_1,S_{T_1})-K_1}.
\]
It is square-integrable in the lognormal model.  By the completeness result
behind \polyref{Prop.~3.3}, or equivalently by applying the BMS PDE to
\((t,x)\mapsto v(T_1-t,x)\) on \(t<T_1\), it admits a self-financing
replication with the money-market account and the stock \(S\) only.  The risky
holding is the delta \(\partial_xv(T_1-t,S_t)\).
\end{solution}
\end{exercise}
